## Requiem Rod

### Stuff that needs to be resolved in order to do this:

1. Perfect Timing
      - This rod has a **unique** thing, you cannot release an input too quickly, otherwise you snap your reel. As I found out on my auto clicker putting `200` ms interval, and hold works, this also gives us a some progress speed, so this is why I mainly want to do this.

2. Minigame Color
      - Requiem doesn't use the normal white bar color, instead it uses a dark green color as its bar color, because of this the macro **cannot** detect the bar. And i am **NOT** planning to add an external feature (shame on you XTernal) to combat this
